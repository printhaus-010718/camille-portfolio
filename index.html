How a project runs

  1. Short call or message. You tell me what you sell, who it's for and what you want visitors to do.
  2. Plan and quote. I map the pages and emails you need and send a fixed scope and price.
  3. Build and review. I build on your platform and share a preview link for your feedback.
  4. Launch and hand over. We go live together and you get a short guide for making your own edits.
